Earth imagery
=============
day.jpg and night.jpg: Solar System Scope, https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
These are the resized textures distributed in the three.js Earth example:
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/webgpu_tsl_earth.html
Original filenames: earth_day_4096.jpg, earth_night_4096.jpg.
No further image modifications; filenames shortened for local hosting.

clouds.png, ocean.jpg, normal.jpg and moon.jpg: three.js example texture collection.
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/textures/planets
Original filenames: earth_clouds_1024.png, earth_specular_2048.jpg,
earth_normal_2048.jpg and moon_1024.jpg. Files are unchanged.

satellite/weather feed. Sun and Moon directions use the site's approximate
astronomical calculation. The Moon's displayed distance is compressed.
